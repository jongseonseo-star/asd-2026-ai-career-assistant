# Candidate evidence and job requirements

Resume strengths must be supported by the selected candidate profile, resume, or stored skill records. A job description states employer requirements; it does not establish that the candidate has those skills. If Docker or automated testing is required by a job but absent from candidate records, describe it as missing evidence, not as a proven inability. Never invent employment, education, qualifications, certifications, technologies or years of experience. This is project-authored review guidance, not evidence about any candidate.
