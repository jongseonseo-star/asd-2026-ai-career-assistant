# Cite resume feedback and show uncertainty

Every feedback claim should point to the actual candidate record, job requirement or retrieved guidance that supports it. Resume guidance cannot establish candidate qualifications. Source identifiers must resolve to supplied evidence; do not invent citations. If evidence is insufficient, say what is missing and avoid a confident claim. Retrieval confidence reflects coverage of the local guidance corpus, not the likelihood of a candidate being hired or the factual accuracy of generated text. This is project-authored validation guidance.
