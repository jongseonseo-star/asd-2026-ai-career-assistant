Analyse the selected candidate profile, resume, and candidate skills against the supplied job description.

First determine what is actually supported by candidate evidence.

Then compare that evidence with the job requirements.

Keep these two concepts separate:

CANDIDATE EVIDENCE
= facts explicitly contained in candidate_profile, resume, or candidate_skills.

JOB REQUIREMENTS
= capabilities requested by the employer.

A job requirement must NEVER be treated as candidate evidence.

Identify:
1. Evidence-based strengths.
2. Requirements not demonstrated by candidate evidence.
3. Realistic actions to address those gaps.
4. Important information that cannot be verified.

Do not rewrite or modify stored candidate data.
